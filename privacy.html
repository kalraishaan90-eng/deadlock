PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: September 14, 2026

1. Offline-First & Local Data Ownership

Dead Lock is built with an offline-first privacy architecture. By default, all training programs, workout completion logs, macronutrient records, and personal biometric measurements (weight, target goals) are saved directly in your browser's localStorage on your physical device. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your workout or fitness data.

2. Camera & Pose Detection Processing

When you activate the Computer Vision Form Check or Automatic Rep Counter, real-time video frames are processed completely on-device using MediaPipe Pose Landmarker in WebAssembly. No video feeds, images, or biometric scan data are ever transmitted to or stored on our servers.

3. Google Authentication & Account Services

If you choose to authenticate using Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication processes your basic public profile (Google user ID, email address, display name, and avatar URL) to authorize access across sessions. You may also use Dead Lock completely anonymously by choosing "Continue as Guest".

4. AI Nutritional Logging (Gemini API)

If you use the AI Food Scanner or Natural Language food logger with your personal Gemini API key, text descriptions or food photos are sent directly to the Google Generative Language API endpoint to estimate macronutrients. No food images are retained by Dead Lock.

5. Data Deletion & Backup Control

You hold 100% control over your data. Under the Profile tab, you can export your complete data as a JSON file at any time or click "Reset All Data" to permanently wipe every record from your device.
